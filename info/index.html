about

we are an architectural practice that takes a holistic approach to the transformation and restoration of existing buildings and urban areas across all scales of the profession, with a particular focus on:

  • transforming our existing surroundings with gentleness, ensuring that new projects create a dynamic and untraditional interplay between historic contexts and new interventions
  • integrating a circular approach to materials, incorporating both biobased and reused material knowledge as well as participating in the development of new tectonic solutions
  • looking beyond our own profession and the project as a standalone work of art to consider how, through collaborative partnerships, we can thoughtfully anchor a project within its physical surroundings

loa was founded in 2020 by architects jan loerakker and lea olsson. our team possesses extensive experience in leading and developing complex projects and can manage every phase of construction, including initial concept studies and analysis, detailed design and execution.

our services

  • urban and architectural design analysis and studies for municipalities, foundations etc.
  • client advising
  • concept proposals and building programs
  • permits and dialogue with authorities
  • detailed design development
  • execution and tendering
  • technical supervision
  • construction and site supervision

clients

hartwig art foundation, coronel foundation, vink bouw, statens kunstfond, arkitekten journal, lomma municipality, creative industries fund, dreyers fond, the royal danish academy

team and collaborators

jan loerakker, amsterdam, 1986, is an architect trained at tu delft and the royal danish academy school of architecture in copenhagen. he has previously worked for crimson architectural historians and gottlieb paludan architects. alongside his practice work, he teaches at the urbanism & societal change master's programme at the royal danish academy school of architecture.

lea olsson, copenhagen, 1986, is an architect maa trained at the royal danish academy school of architecture in copenhagen, and the aa school of architecture in london. she has previously worked for oma and tegnestuen vandkunsten, and taught at the royal danish academy school of architecture.
